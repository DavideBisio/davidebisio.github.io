[`DavideBisio/claude-code-workflow`](https://github.com/DavideBisio/claude-code-workflow) is a public repo where I'm experimenting with AI-assisted development workflows.

## Why I'm building this

I work on AI workflow development, and this is an example project to try new things out in that space.

## What I wish to do

- Build a development companion that fits into day-to-day coding
- Automate repetitive workflow steps
- Ensure a workflow actually runs to completion, not just partway
- Guide the AI toward my goals instead of generic output
- Optimize the whole loop over time

## What I did

So far: scaffolded the idea. It's an early skeleton, not a finished tool.

## Todo

- Compare against other solutions in this space
- Iterate and improve based on what the scaffold reveals

## Pros and cons, so far

**Pros**

- Fast to get a working skeleton in place
- Forces me to be explicit about what "workflow completion" should mean
- Good sandbox for testing ideas before committing to a design

**Cons**

- Still unproven against real alternatives
- Scaffold-stage: lots of rough edges and open questions
