      right at the base of the climb to Monte Maccione, in a quiet parking spot along
      the road.

    places: ["Oliena", "Punta Corrasi"]
    activities: ["Hiking to Punta Corrasi"]
    notes: |
      Hiking to Punta Corrasi: awesome panoramic view from there. No particular notes

  - date: 2026-09-14
    title: "Punta Cusidore hike"
    places: ["Oliena", "Punta Cusidore", "Orgoi", "Su Golgone"]
    activities: ["Hiking to Punta Cusidore"]
    notes: |
      Hiking to Punta Cusidore: the same initial path as the day before, now turning left
      on a sun exposed high plane, with many caves and ancient sheepfolds. The path is
      tricky but not too steep, until the base of Punta Cusidore, where I explored Orgoi
      cave. The way back is on the opposite side of the mountain I just sided before.
      This section is more demanding due to the extreme steepness of the track. On the
      evening I supplied some groceries, then moved to Su Golgone. Here I spent the night
      on a parking by the entrance of the site, ready for the next day hike.
  - date: 2026-09-15
    title: "Fruncu Nieddu hike"
    places: ["Su Golgone", "Ovile Biseddu", "Fruncu Nieddu", "Sovana"]
    activities: ["Hiking to Fruncu Nieddu"]
    notes: |
      Hiking to Fruncu Nieddu: starting from Su Golgone, I first headed to Ovile Biseddu.
      After that point the path is extremely steep and difficult up to the summit of
      Fruncu Nieddu. It require good skills, following the piles of stones which are the
      only waypoints that serve as a guide. The track is on the bare rock and it is not
      visible otherwise. Past this, the hike continue on good trails to Sovana, then way
      back to Su Golgone. I spent the evening and the night on a free camping spot past
      Su Golgone, towards Lanaitho.

    notes: |

      slept near to the sports field.
